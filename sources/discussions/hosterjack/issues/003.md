Mate... you can't do this.

My repo is one of the two you say this "merges." I built it by hand and have kept it (mostly) up-to-date, and cross-checked various community solutions and hints to ensure they are reproducible. You copied all of it into a non-fork, released it as CC0, and put your donation address on it. Not Cool.

The whole point of me releasing my github repo was to be a place where people could contribute back; and that's the whole point of Github. It even has a way to do this: forking. You get to keep the history, lineage, and attribution so that disputes like this one don't really happen. That's how my repo started and why it exists; it's a fork of puzzlehunt's and I got PRs merged before they went AFK. 

And the CC0 is just invalid. My repo has no license, which means all rights reserved by default. Crediting me in the README isn't permission because you can't just put other people's work into public domain. (And you can't put the artwork/images from GSMG into the public domain).

Your static search site is nice, albeit a bit overwhelming, but on the right track for something useful. It would have been a welcome PR to organize the static content into something searchable. Especially for non-verified paths that so far haven't led anywhere. Instead we now have a third divergent copy calling itself "the authoritative" source.

So, either make this a proper fork, remove what you copied from my repo, or at minimum drop the CC0 and donation address. But, I'd love if you just actually contributed back, github is supposed to be a community platform and I started my repo to be that place.
